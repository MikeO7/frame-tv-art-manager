## 2026-05-26 - Integer Math and Fixed Arrays for Saliency Queue
**Learning:** In highly-iterative image saliency map generation algorithms, dynamically appending to slices incurs significant underlying re-allocation and GC overhead. Floating-point division and multiplication inside loop paths are slow.
**Action:** Always pre-allocate the queue to its maximum theoretical size `w*h` with integer indexing pointers `head` and `tail` instead of utilizing dynamically sized slices to avoid memory movement in memory-intensive operations. Refactor inner-loop float operations into `(R*299+G*587+B*114)/1000` integer equations.
