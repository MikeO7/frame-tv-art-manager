Free, self-hosted art sync for Samsung The Frame

Your own art on Samsung The Frame. No subscription, no cloud.

Frame TV Art Manager keeps your Frame TV's Art Mode in sync with a folder on your NAS or home server. Drop in photos or public-domain paintings; it prepares each one for the 4K screen, shows portraits whole on a gallery mat instead of cropping them, and uploads to every Frame in the house over your local network.

How it works

You manage a folder; Frame TV Art Manager keeps every Frame matched to it.

  1. Add pictures

    Copy JPEG or PNG files into the artwork folder, send them from your iPhone, or let it fetch art from NASA and museum collections.

  2. It prepares each one

    Upright, color-corrected, sharpened, and fitted to 3840×2160: cropped only when little is lost, otherwise shown whole on a gallery mat.

  3. Every TV stays in sync

    New pictures are uploaded once, deleted ones are removed, and a full TV takes turns. Your own uploads are never touched.

Portraits shown whole, not cropped at the neck

The Frame is a 16:9 landscape. Filling it with a portrait means cutting off the top and bottom, which is where heads are. By default, Frame TV Art Manager sets portraits and square art on a deep charcoal mat, faintly tinted toward the picture's colors and calm in a dark room at night.

Built to run unattended for months

Never uploads twice

Every picture is identified by a hash of its contents, so the same photo is never sent to the TV again, even after a rename or re-copy.

Every Frame in the house

List each TV's address in TV_IPS. They share one collection and are tracked separately, so one TV being off doesn't hold up the rest.

Prepared for the 4K screen

Turned upright from EXIF, color profiles converted to sRGB, HDR tone-mapped, resized in linear light to 3840×2160, and finished with halo-free contrast-adaptive sharpening.

Gallery mat, not a crop

Portraits, square art, panoramas, and small images are shown whole on a calm charcoal mat tinted from the art. See examples.

Portrait collages

Optionally pair two portraits on one screen, matched by color, lightness, and shape, in a double-opening mat.

Rotates when the TV is full

Each piece stays 14 days, then makes room for art not yet shown. How rotation works.

Art from anywhere

A shared folder, a password-protected upload page, iPhone Shortcuts, or online sources: NASA, the Art Institute of Chicago, Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, and direct links.

Samsung mattes too

Set one matte for everything with MATTE_STYLE, or per picture in mattes.json. The default is full screen.

TV controls, if you want them

Slideshow timing, brightness that follows the sun, a nightly power-off, and Wake-on-LAN. Each stays off until you set it.

Coming soon: a built-in MCP server, so AI assistants such as Claude or ChatGPT can put together themed sets, like a Christmas collection of public-domain museum paintings, and send them to the TV without API keys. It isn't available yet.

Why Frame owners look for something else

Subscriptions

Samsung's Art Store costs US$4.99 a month in the US (October 2026), and some owners report being asked to subscribe for a slideshow of their own photos. Your own art and public-domain paintings need neither. Art without a subscription

One photo at a time

Phone uploads are fine for a few pictures, but tedious for a collection or several TVs. A folder sync does it for you. Automatic uploads

Duplicates and full storage

Scripts that re-upload everything fill the TV with copies until it refuses more. This never sends a picture twice and rotates when full. Storage full guide

Cloud dependence

Samsung announced paid SmartThings API access in June 2026. Frame TV Art Manager works entirely on your local network. Compare the options

Careful by default

Software that deletes things from your TV should be predictable. These are the rules it follows out of the box.

It will

  • Upload new pictures on the next sync.
  • Remove a piece it uploaded once you delete it from the folder.
  • Swap its own pieces on a full TV, every 14 days by default.
  • Replace each picture in the folder with its prepared version, so keep originals elsewhere.

It won't

  • Delete art you added yourself, unless you set REMOVE_UNKNOWN_IMAGES=true.
  • Change anything on a TV whose state it can't confirm.
  • Empty the TV if the folder is emptied, or drop art because a website is down.
  • Choose which piece is on screen, or change anything at all with DRY_RUN=true.

Set it up in minutes

The only required setting is your TV's IP address. Approve the connection once on the TV, then add pictures to data/artwork.

services:
  frame-tv-art-manager:
    image: ghcr.io/mikeo7/frame-tv-art-manager:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    environment:
      TV_IPS: "192.168.1.150"
    ports:
      - "8080:8080"
    volumes:
      - ./data:/data

Frequently asked questions

What is Frame TV Art Manager?

Frame TV Art Manager is a free, self-hosted service for Samsung The Frame owners. It runs in Docker on a NAS or home server and keeps the TV's Art Mode in sync with a folder of JPEG and PNG pictures, preparing each one for the 3840×2160 screen and uploading it over your home network.

Do I need a Samsung Art Store subscription?

No. Frame TV Art Manager uploads your own pictures and free art you choose, which don't need the Art Store. It doesn't unlock or touch Samsung's paid catalog.

Do I need SmartThings or a Samsung account?

No. It talks to the TV directly on your local network. Only optional online sources such as NASA or Unsplash contact the internet, and only when you turn them on.

How does it handle portrait photos?

By default portraits are shown whole on a deep charcoal gallery mat, faintly tinted toward the picture's colors, instead of being cropped. You can also pair two portraits side by side, matched by color, or crop to fill the screen.

Will it delete art I already put on my TV?

No. It only removes art it uploaded itself. Art you added with SmartThings, a USB stick, or anything else stays unless you deliberately set REMOVE_UNKNOWN_IMAGES=true.

What happens when my Frame TV's storage is full?

It rotates the art it uploaded: each piece stays at least 14 days by default, then is swapped for art that hasn't been shown yet. Your own uploads and the files in your folder are never deleted.

Will it upload the same photo twice?

No. Pictures are recognized by their contents, so a photo that is already on the TV is never sent again, even under a new name.

What can I run it on?

Anything that runs Docker and stays on: a Synology, Unraid, or TrueNAS NAS, a mini PC, a Raspberry Pi-class board, or a Mac. The image is built for amd64, arm64, and armv7.

Which Frame TVs does it work with?

Samsung Frame TVs running Tizen 8.0 or newer. Older firmware may work, but Samsung's private Art Mode protocol changes between models and releases. Several TVs can be managed at once.

Is Frame TV Art Manager free and open source?

It is free for personal and other noncommercial use under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. The code is public, but commercial use isn't permitted, so it is source-available rather than open source. It is not affiliated with Samsung.
