iPhone and Apple Photos

How to send photos from an iPhone or Apple Photos to a Samsung Frame TV

Short answer: turn on Frame TV Art Manager's upload page and send photos to it from your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. The quickest way is the upload page in Safari; an iPhone Shortcut or a Mac script can send favorites or an album on a schedule. Photos join the artwork folder and reach every Frame TV on the next sync, within 30 minutes by default.

By MikeO7Published July 26, 2026Last updated November 6, 2026

Photos travel from your device to your own server over your home network. Whether your Photos library uses iCloud is up to your Apple settings; Frame TV Art Manager doesn't touch it. SERVER-IP below means the machine running Frame TV Art Manager, not the TV.

Turn on the uploader

The uploader is off by default. Add two settings to your compose file and restart the container:

environment:
  TV_IPS: "192.168.1.150"
  UPLOAD_ENABLED: "true"
  UPLOAD_TOKEN: "a-long-random-password-of-16-plus-characters"
ports:
  - "8080:8080"

Sign in with username frame and your UPLOAD_TOKEN as the password. Each upload can be up to 20 MB (MAX_DOWNLOAD_SIZE_MB).

Quickest: the upload page in Safari

Open http://SERVER-IP:8080/upload in Safari, sign in, tap the drop area, and choose photos. The page sends them one by one and shows each result. On a Mac you can drag files onto it. It accepts JPEG and PNG; if a HEIC photo is rejected, use the Shortcut below, which converts explicitly.

Build an iPhone or iPad Shortcut

First make the sign-in value the Shortcut needs. On a Mac or Linux computer, run this with your own token and copy the output:

printf '%s' 'frame:YOUR_UPLOAD_TOKEN' | base64
  1. Create the shortcut. In Shortcuts, tap +, and name it "Send to Frame TV".
  2. Find the photos. Add Find Photos, filter for Favorites or an album, add Media Type is Image, and set a small Limit while testing.
  3. Loop over them. Add Repeat with Each.
  4. Convert to JPEG. Inside the loop, add Convert Image to JPEG. iPhone photos are usually HEIC, which the uploader doesn't accept.
  5. Upload each photo. Add Get Contents of URL: URL http://SERVER-IP:8080/upload, method POST, request body Form with a File field named file, and a header Authorization set to Basic, a space, and your encoded value.
  6. Test it. Each photo should return "File imported successfully", or "File already exists (deduplicated)" if it was sent before.

To run it on a schedule, use the Automation tab: Time of Day, run immediately, and pick the shortcut. Identical files are skipped, but if iOS produces a slightly different JPEG of the same photo on a later run, it's stored as a new picture, so keep the album focused.

Send favorites from a Mac automatically

The free third-party tool osxphotos exports favorites or an album as JPEGs, and a short script uploads them. Give your terminal Full Disk Access in System Settings, install osxphotos (for example pipx install osxphotos), and save this as ~/frame-tv-sync.sh:

#!/bin/bash
set -euo pipefail
SERVER="http://192.168.1.20:8080"     # the Frame TV Art Manager server, not the TV
UPLOAD_TOKEN="your-upload-token"
EXPORT_DIR="$HOME/Pictures/frame-tv-export"
export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:/opt/homebrew/bin:/usr/local/bin:$PATH"
mkdir -p "$EXPORT_DIR"
osxphotos export "$EXPORT_DIR" --favorite \
  --convert-to-jpeg --jpeg-quality 0.95 \
  --skip-live --skip-raw --update --cleanup
for file in "$EXPORT_DIR"/*.jpeg "$EXPORT_DIR"/*.jpg; do
  [ -f "$file" ] || continue
  curl --fail --silent --show-error -u "frame:$UPLOAD_TOKEN" \
    -F "file=@$file" "$SERVER/upload"
  echo
done

Replace --favorite with --album "Frame TV" to send an album. Run it hourly with a LaunchAgent whose StartInterval is 3600; if it works by hand but not on schedule, grant Full Disk Access to /bin/bash.

Upload a photo with curl

curl -u "frame:$UPLOAD_TOKEN" -F "file=@/path/to/photo.jpg" http://SERVER-IP:8080/upload

The form field must be named file. Send files one at a time: a second upload sent at the same moment gets HTTP 429.

What happens after a photo is uploaded

On the next sync it's prepared like any other picture: turned upright, shown whole on a gallery mat if it's a portrait, converted to sRGB, sharpened, and resized to 3840×2160. Then it goes to every TV in TV_IPS. To take it off the TV, delete its file from the artwork folder; the upload response tells you its name.

Upload errors

Responses from /upload
ResponseMeaning and fix
401 Authentication requiredWrong username or password. The username is always frame.
403 Upload endpoint is disabledSet UPLOAD_ENABLED=true and restart. Uploads are also off during DRY_RUN.
400 file too largeOver MAX_DOWNLOAD_SIZE_MB (20 MB by default).
400 Invalid or unsafe imageNot a complete JPEG or PNG. HEIC photos land here; convert to JPEG first.
400 missing 'file' parameterThe form field isn't named file.
429 Another artwork import is in progressTwo uploads at once. Send them one after another.

Keep the uploader private

  • It's off until you set UPLOAD_ENABLED=true, and every request needs the password.
  • Plain HTTP doesn't encrypt the password or photos. Keep it on your home network or behind a TLS reverse proxy.
  • Never forward port 8080 from your router to the internet.
  • Uploads are checked to be complete images, and original filenames are never used on the server.

Questions people ask

How do I add photos to a Samsung Frame TV from an iPhone?

Samsung's way is the SmartThings app. With Frame TV Art Manager, open its upload page in Safari or use an iPhone Shortcut; photos reach every Frame TV on the next sync.

Why won't my iPhone photos upload?

iPhone photos are usually HEIC, which the uploader doesn't accept. Convert them to JPEG first, for example with the Convert Image action in Shortcuts.

Can I sync an Apple Photos album to The Frame automatically?

Yes. A scheduled iPhone Shortcut or a Mac script with osxphotos can send an album or your favorites on a timer. Photos already sent are recognized and skipped.
