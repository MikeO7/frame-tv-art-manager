Troubleshooting

Samsung Frame TV art troubleshooting

Short answer: most problems come down to three things: the TV hasn't approved the connection, the TV was off or not in Art Mode during the sync, or its storage is full. Check http://SERVER-IP:8080/status; each TV's status says which it is, and the sections below explain each one.

The TV never shows an Allow/Deny prompt

  • Make sure the TV is on and in Art Mode, and that the server can reach it (ping TV-IP).
  • Check TV_IPS matches the TV's current address, and give the TV a DHCP reservation so it doesn't change.
  • If you pressed Deny before, remove the entry from the TV's device connection settings and restart the container.
  • Keep /data/tokens across upgrades. Without the saved token, the TV asks again.

Pictures don't appear on the TV

  • Wait for the next sync (30 minutes by default), or restart the container to sync now.
  • Only .jpg, .jpeg, and .png are used. Hidden files, temporary files, JSON, YAML, and symlinks are ignored.
  • Look in Art Mode under My Photos; uploaded pictures appear there, not in the Art Store.
  • The manager changes the picture every 15 minutes by default, so a new picture comes up in its turn, and only while the TV is in Art Mode. With SLIDESHOW_ENABLED=false or SLIDESHOW_MODE=tv, the TV decides what is shown.

"Recovery required" won't clear

To avoid duplicates or wrong deletions, that TV is paused until the result can be confirmed. If it doesn't clear on later syncs, stop the container and delete that TV's tv_*_reconciliation.json file in the tokens folder. The manager then forgets which art it uploaded: existing art stays on the TV as if you'd added it yourself, and your collection is uploaded again.

It stops at startup after an upgrade

Invalid settings stop the service at startup with a clear error in the log, so a typo can't silently change behavior. Two changes catch people after upgrading:

  • On/off values are strict. They accept true/false, 1/0, yes/no, on/off, or y/n in any case. Anything else, such as enabled, stops startup.
  • Brightness uses the Frame's 0–10 scale. Values from 11 to 50, from the 0–50 scale earlier releases documented, are converted (30 becomes 6) and logged as a warning; anything else outside 0–10 stops startup.

Also note that TV_MAC on its own no longer wakes the TV; add AUTO_ON_TIME, for example 07:00.

The picture now changes every 15 minutes by default. Set SLIDESHOW_ENABLED=false to stop that, or SLIDESHOW_MODE=tv to go back to the TV's own slideshow; with SLIDESHOW_MODE=tv, a SLIDESHOW_INTERVAL other than 3, 15, 60, 720, or 1440 stops startup.

/status shows redacted or source_warning

  • "redacted": true, TVs named tv-1, no error text: UPLOAD_TOKEN is set, and the request didn't sign in. Open /status signed in as frame with that token to see addresses and errors. Wrong credentials get 401.
  • source_warning: an online art source failed during the sync. Nothing it downloaded before is removed, and the TVs still sync the art already on disk. The source is tried again later, after its rate-limit reset or a growing delay of 15 minutes up to a day.

Seasonal art doesn't appear, or everyday art disappeared

  • New collections start off. Give the collection a schedule, such as a window from 12-01 to 01-06, or always.
  • Dates follow LOCATION_TIMEZONE, UTC by default, so a window may open hours earlier or later than you expect.
  • Everyday art gone? An active exclusive collection replaces it until its window ends. Make the collection non-exclusive to show both.
  • Sync reported as failed? .frame-tv-art-manager/collections.json may have a typo or the wrong permissions; it must be readable only by the service user (chmod 600). Until it's fixed, the manager keeps the last collections it read and changes nothing based on a guess.

The seasonal collections guide explains schedules in full.

Art looks cropped, blurry, or too bright

  • Cropped portraits: the default PORTRAIT_MODE=pad shows them whole; see the portrait guide.
  • Blurry: the source is too small for 4K; see the art size guide.
  • Too bright or dim at night: set BRIGHTNESS on the Frame's 0–10 scale, or SOLAR_BRIGHTNESS_ENABLED=true to follow the sun. A TV that doesn't answer brightness or slideshow requests keeps syncing art; that setting is skipped with a warning in the log.
  • Wrong crop or mat after changing a setting: the next sync re-prepares every picture from its kept original, so the new setting applies cleanly. Pictures prepared by earlier versions, which replaced originals outright, can't get back what was cropped away; delete the prepared file and add the original again.
  • Left on all night: AUTO_OFF_TIME turns off TVs showing art at a set time.

Permission errors on /data

Create the folders yourself with mkdir -p data/artwork data/tokens, then set PUID and PGID to the owner you want, or own the folders and add user: "UID:GID" to the service with PUID/PGID unset.

Upload page errors

HEIC photos, wrong passwords, and parallel uploads cause most upload errors. The Apple Photos guide lists every response and its fix.

Still stuck?

Open an issue on GitHub with your TV model, firmware version, and the relevant log lines. Logs are JSON; errors from the TV include error_kind, operation, and retryable fields. Set LOG_LEVEL=debug for more detail.

Questions people ask

Why isn't my Frame TV showing the Allow prompt?

The TV must be on, in Art Mode, and reachable from the server. If you denied it before, remove the entry from the TV's device connection settings and restart the container.

Why is my Frame TV skipped during sync?

It was off or showing other content. Frame TV Art Manager never changes a TV whose state it can't confirm, and tries again on the next sync.

Why don't new pictures show on the TV?

They are added to My Photos on the next sync and come up in turn: the manager changes the picture every 15 minutes by default while the TV is in Art Mode. With SLIDESHOW_ENABLED=false or SLIDESHOW_MODE=tv, the TV decides what is shown.

Why did my everyday art disappear from the Frame TV?

An exclusive themed collection, such as Christmas, is probably active. While it is, only its pictures are shown; the everyday art returns when its date window ends. Your files are never deleted.
