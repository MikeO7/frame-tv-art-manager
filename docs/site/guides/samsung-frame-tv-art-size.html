Image size

Samsung Frame TV art size: 3840×2160 explained

Short answer: make art for The Frame at 3840×2160 pixels, a 16:9 landscape, in the sRGB color space, saved as a high-quality JPEG. That matches the 4K panel exactly, so the TV doesn't have to crop or scale. The 32-inch Frame has a 1920×1080 panel. Frame TV Art Manager prepares every picture to this size automatically: it rotates it upright, converts its colors to sRGB, and crops it or sets it on a gallery mat to fit.

Quick reference

Recommended image specification for Samsung The Frame
Pixel size3840×2160 (4K models); 1920×1080 for the 32-inch model
Aspect ratio16:9 landscape
Color spacesRGB
File formatJPEG, high quality; PNG also works
Not acceptedHEIC (iPhone default), RAW, TIFF, WebP: convert to JPEG first

Check your model's specifications if you're unsure of its panel resolution. To prepare everything at 1080p, set IMAGE_MAX_WIDTH=1920 and IMAGE_MAX_HEIGHT=1080.

What happens to each shape

Frame TV Art Manager fills the screen only when cropping removes at most 25% of the picture (IMAGE_MAX_CROP_PERCENT). Anything else is shown whole on a gallery mat.

Small images and blur

Enlarging a small image can't add detail; it just makes it soft. Frame TV Art Manager fills the screen only when that needs at most 3× enlargement (IMAGE_MAX_UPSCALE). A 1200×800 photo, for example, would need 3.2× and is shown whole on a mat instead. Sharpening is also skipped on images enlarged more than 1.5×, because there's no detail to recover and sharpening would only exaggerate the blur.

Color: why sRGB

The Frame expects standard sRGB color. Pictures from cameras and editing apps often carry a different color profile, such as Display P3 or Adobe RGB; shown without conversion, they look dull or oversaturated. Frame TV Art Manager converts any embedded ICC color profile to sRGB, and tone-maps HDR PNGs to standard range so bright skies don't clip.

How Frame TV Art Manager prepares each picture

  1. Rotates it upright using its EXIF orientation, so phone photos aren't sideways.
  2. Decides between a crop and a gallery mat using the rules above.
  3. Converts its color to sRGB and tone-maps HDR PNGs.
  4. Resizes it in linear light, which keeps fine bright detail such as stars or lace from darkening, to exactly 3840×2160.
  5. Applies halo-free, contrast-adaptive sharpening (skipped on images enlarged more than 1.5×).
  6. Saves JPEG at quality 95 with full 4:4:4 color, so red and blue edges stay crisp (IMAGE_JPEG_SUBSAMPLING=420 makes smaller files); PNGs stay PNG, including transparency.

A 24-megapixel camera photo takes about a second. The prepared file replaces the picture in the artwork folder, and the untouched original is kept in its hidden .frame-tv-art-manager/originals directory, so changing a setting later re-prepares the art from the original without losing quality. Originals take about as much disk space as the pictures you add.

File size and limits

A 3840×2160 JPEG at quality 95 is usually a few megabytes. Downloads and web uploads are limited to 20 MB by default (MAX_DOWNLOAD_SIZE_MB, 1–100). Files you copy into the artwork folder may be up to 200 MB (MAX_LOCAL_FILE_SIZE_MB, 1–512); larger ones are skipped with a warning. Each image is processed within a 512 MB memory budget, which is enough for an 8-bit 8K original, and images above 40 megapixels are left out with a warning in the log.

Making art with an AI image tool? Ask for a 16:9 landscape at the highest resolution it offers, ideally exactly 3840×2160; anything smaller than about 2560×1440 is visibly enlarged on the 4K screen. The AI art guide shows how an assistant connected over MCP reads this spec itself.

Questions people ask

What size should pictures be for a Samsung Frame TV?

3840×2160 pixels for the 4K models, a 16:9 landscape. The 32-inch Frame has a 1920×1080 panel.

What aspect ratio is The Frame?

16:9. Pictures with other shapes are cropped or shown with a border.

Should Frame TV art be JPEG or PNG?

High-quality JPEG in sRGB is the safest choice. PNG also works. iPhone HEIC photos must be converted to JPEG first.

Why does my art look blurry on The Frame?

Usually because a small image was enlarged to fill the 4K screen. Use images at least 3840 pixels wide, or let Frame TV Art Manager show small images whole on a mat instead of blowing them up.

Do I need to resize photos myself?

Not with Frame TV Art Manager. It rotates, color-converts, and resizes every picture to 3840×2160 automatically.
