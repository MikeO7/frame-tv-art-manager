SmartThings alternative for Samsung Frame TV art

Short answer: if you add a photo now and then, Samsung's SmartThings app is the simplest way. Frame TV Art Manager is a local alternative for people with a NAS or home server who want The Frame to follow a folder automatically, with several TVs, large collections, portraits shown whole, and rotation when the TV is full. You can use both.

SmartThings and its API

The SmartThings phone app stays free. Separately, in June 2026 Samsung announced paid access to the SmartThings API, which tools and smart-home integrations use: a US$4.99-a-month personal plan, with free access through the third quarter of 2026. Frame TV Art Manager doesn't use SmartThings or its API; it talks to the TV on your local network.

Some owners have also reported since July 2025 that the "Display All" slideshow of My Photos asked for an Art Store subscription (forum thread); Samsung's help pages still describe it as free. See Frame TV art without a subscription.

Other community tools

Several community projects also work with The Frame, including Home Assistant integrations and Python libraries for Samsung's Art Mode API. They suit different needs: some focus on remote control, others are building blocks for your own scripts. When choosing one for unattended use, check how it handles duplicates, whether its cleanup can delete pictures you uploaded by hand, and what it does when the TV is off or unreachable.

Choose Frame TV Art Manager when

  • You already run a NAS, home server, mini PC, or Mac that stays on.
  • Your art lives in a folder, or you'd like it to.
  • Your collection is bigger than the TV holds and should rotate.
  • You have more than one Frame.
  • You shoot portraits and want them shown whole.
  • You'd like museum or NASA art to arrive on its own.

Choose SmartThings when

  • You only add a photo now and then.
  • You don't want a computer running all the time.
  • You prefer an official app with Samsung support.

Using both

Frame TV Art Manager only manages pieces it uploaded. Anything you add through SmartThings or USB stays on the TV and is never rotated out, unless you deliberately set REMOVE_UNKNOWN_IMAGES=true. Those pieces do take up space, which leaves less room for rotation.

Why not a simple upload script?

Uploading files is easy. Keeping a TV matched to a folder for months without mistakes is harder: knowing which pieces you uploaded and which you didn't, not treating an unreachable TV or failed folder scan as "delete everything", coping with a full TV, and surviving a power cut mid-write. Frame TV Art Manager keeps checksummed per-TV records, writes them so a crash can't leave a half-written file, and makes no changes when it can't confirm what's on the TV.

Questions people ask

Is there an alternative to SmartThings for Frame TV art?

Yes. A USB stick works without an account, and Frame TV Art Manager uploads from a folder on your home network without SmartThings or a Samsung account.

Does the SmartThings API price change affect Frame TV Art Manager?

No. Frame TV Art Manager talks to the TV directly on your local network and doesn't use the SmartThings API.

Can I use SmartThings and Frame TV Art Manager together?

Yes. Frame TV Art Manager only manages the pieces it uploaded, so photos you add through SmartThings stay on the TV.
