Seasonal collections

Seasonal and holiday art collections on the Samsung Frame TV

Short answer: Frame TV Art Manager can show Christmas, Halloween, or spring art on The Frame only on the dates you choose. Put the pictures in a themed collection with a yearly window, such as December 1 to January 6. While the window is open, the collection replaces your everyday art (exclusive, the default) or is shown alongside it. When the window closes, the holiday pictures come off the TV and your everyday art returns by itself. No files are deleted, and art you added to the TV yourself is never touched.

How a themed collection works

A themed collection is a named group of pictures from your artwork folder, such as "Christmas", that is shown only when its schedule says so. Without any collections nothing changes: every picture in the folder is part of the normal rotation. Each collection has one of three schedules:

Themed collection schedules
ScheduleWhen it is active
alwaysAlways.
offNever. New collections start here, so nothing changes on the TV until you schedule one.
windowEvery year from a start date to an end date, written as MM-DD and including both days. A window can run across New Year, such as 12-01 to 01-06. 02-29 is allowed and simply doesn't occur in other years.

Dates follow LOCATION_TIMEZONE, the same clock as AUTO_OFF_TIME (UTC by default), so a window opens at midnight where the TV is. The change reaches the TV on the first sync after midnight, within 30 minutes by default.

Replace your everyday art, or add to it

Pictures that belong to no collection are your everyday art. Each sync picks what to show like this:

  • Exclusive (the default): while an exclusive collection is active and holds at least one picture, only pictures from active collections are shown. Your Christmas art replaces the everyday art.
  • Not exclusive: an active collection is shown together with the everyday art. Good for a light seasonal touch, such as spring flowers mixed into your usual photos.
  • Out of season: a picture that belongs only to inactive collections is hidden, and its copy is removed from the TV like any picture that left the folder. A picture in several collections is shown while any of them is active.

Hiding a picture never deletes its file. The manager's other rules still apply: it never removes art you put on the TV yourself, and it never chooses which stored piece is on screen; the TV and its slideshow do that.

A year of art: Christmas, Halloween, and spring

Three collections cover most of a year: Christmas, exclusive, from 12-01 to 01-06; Halloween, exclusive, from 10-01 to 10-31; and Spring, not exclusive, from 03-20 to 06-20. Here is what the TV shows on a few dates:

What the Frame shows with three seasonal collections
DateActiveOn the TV
April 10SpringEveryday art plus the spring pictures
October 15HalloweenHalloween pictures only
November 7NoneEveryday art only; all seasonal pictures hidden
December 24ChristmasChristmas pictures only
January 7NoneEveryday art only

An exclusive collection with no pictures in the folder doesn't replace anything, so an empty Halloween collection leaves your everyday art up.

Create and schedule a collection

With an AI assistant

The easiest way is an AI assistant connected over MCP. Ask for "a Christmas collection shown December 1 to January 6" and it adds the pictures, then sets the schedule with update_collection and starts a sync. It can make new images or find public-domain paintings. The AI art guide shows the setup and a full example.

By hand

Collections live in .frame-tv-art-manager/collections.json inside the artwork folder, for example data/artwork/.frame-tv-art-manager/collections.json. The file must be readable only by the service user (chmod 600). Each member names a picture by its key; the assistant's list_artwork tool reports every picture's key.

{
  "version": 1,
  "collections": [
    {
      "slug": "christmas",
      "name": "Christmas",
      "exclusive": true,
      "activation": { "mode": "window", "start": "12-01", "end": "01-06" },
      "members": [
        { "key": "snowy-canal.jpg", "added_at": "2026-11-07T00:00:00Z" }
      ]
    },
    {
      "slug": "halloween",
      "name": "Halloween",
      "exclusive": true,
      "activation": { "mode": "window", "start": "10-01", "end": "10-31" },
      "members": []
    },
    {
      "slug": "spring",
      "name": "Spring",
      "exclusive": false,
      "activation": { "mode": "window", "start": "03-20", "end": "06-20" },
      "members": []
    }
  ]
}

Membership follows each picture's stable identity, so renaming a picture, or re-preparing it after a settings change, keeps it in its collection. One limitation: two portraits joined into a side-by-side collage become a new picture that belongs to no collection.

Fill a collection with public-domain paintings

Museums hold centuries of winter scenes, harvests, and spring gardens, free to use. Two ways to bring them in:

  • Into a collection, through your assistant. Its search_public_domain_art tool searches the Art Institute of Chicago, The Met, the Cleveland Museum of Art, and SMK at once. Pick the works you want and add_artwork stores each one in the collection with its title, artist, license, and museum link.
  • Into your everyday art, through a sources file. A line such as museum:search:christmas downloads up to 20 public-domain works mixed across the same four museums. Downloads join the everyday art, which shows all year, so add them to a collection afterwards if they should be seasonal.

The free art guide covers every museum source, search filters, and credits.

Collections on a full TV

Themed collections decide which pictures are wanted today. Rotation and MAX_ARTWORK_IMAGES then work on those pictures only.

  • When an exclusive collection opens, the everyday pieces the manager uploaded are no longer wanted, so they are removed from the TV and the holiday pictures are uploaded in their place. When it closes, the reverse happens and the everyday art is uploaded again. Expect a busy sync at both ends of the window.
  • If the collection is bigger than the TV holds, it rotates within itself: each piece stays at least ROTATION_INTERVAL_DAYS (14 by default). A five-week Christmas window shows about three rounds at 14 days; set a shorter interval, such as 3, if you want more of the set to appear.
  • A non-exclusive collection on a full TV waits its turn: its pictures join the queue, and pieces never shown go first, but a slot only opens when a piece on the TV has stayed its full interval.
  • Art you uploaded to the TV yourself is never rotated or removed, and it takes up space the collections can't use.

If the collections file has a mistake

If collections.json can't be read, for example after a typo, the manager never shows or removes art based on a guess. It keeps the last collections it read successfully since it started and reports the sync as failed in /status and /health until you fix the file. If it has never read the file, it skips the TV work for that sync and reports the error. Tools that change collections refuse to overwrite a file they can't read, and with DRY_RUN=true collections are evaluated but never saved.

Questions people ask

Can I show Christmas art on my Frame TV only in December?

Yes. Put the pictures in a themed collection with a yearly window, such as 12-01 to 12-31 or 12-01 to 01-06. Frame TV Art Manager shows them only while the window is open, in your LOCATION_TIMEZONE.

What happens to my everyday art during the holidays?

With an exclusive collection, the default, the everyday pieces the manager uploaded come off the TV while the holiday collection is active and go back up when it ends. With a non-exclusive collection, both are shown.

Are holiday pictures deleted after the season?

No. Their copies are removed from the TV, but the files stay in your artwork folder and come back the next year when the window opens again.

Can a collection run over New Year?

Yes. A window whose end date is earlier than its start date, such as 12-01 to 01-06, wraps the new year. Both the start and end days are included.

Do I need an AI assistant to use themed collections?

No. You can edit .frame-tv-art-manager/collections.json in the artwork folder by hand. An assistant connected over MCP just makes it easier to add pictures and set dates.
