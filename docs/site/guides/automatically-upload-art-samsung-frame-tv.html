Automatic uploads

How to upload photos to a Samsung Frame TV automatically

Short answer: run Frame TV Art Manager on a NAS or home server, give it your Frame TV's IP address, and put JPEG or PNG files in its artwork folder. It uploads new pictures at startup and every 30 minutes, removes ones you delete from the folder, never uploads the same picture twice, and leaves art you added yourself alone. No SmartThings app or Samsung account is needed.

Every way to get photos onto The Frame

Ways to upload your own photos to a Samsung Frame TV
MethodGood forDrawbacks
SmartThings appA few photos from your phoneOne batch at a time, by hand, per TV; a Samsung account
USB stickA one-off batch without a phoneWalking to the TV for every change
Frame TV Art ManagerA collection that changes, several TVs, large librariesNeeds an always-on computer running Docker

The first two are Samsung's built-in options. This guide covers the third: a folder that the TV follows on its own.

What "automatic" means here

Frame TV Art Manager treats a folder as the list of art that should be on your Frame. At startup and every 30 minutes after (SYNC_INTERVAL_MINUTES), it compares the folder with what's stored on each TV, uploads anything missing, and removes pieces it uploaded that you have since deleted from the folder. You never open an app.

What you need

  • A Samsung Frame TV running Tizen 8.0 or newer, on your home network.
  • An always-on machine on the same network that runs Docker: a NAS, Unraid server, mini PC, Raspberry Pi-class board, or Mac.
  • A fixed IP address for each TV, set with a DHCP reservation in your router.
  • Pictures as JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg) or PNG (.png). iPhone HEIC photos need converting first; the Apple Photos guide shows how.

The Docker guide walks through installation. The only required setting is TV_IPS.

How each sync works

  1. Download anything new from your online sources, if you set any up.
  2. Check the folder and prepare pictures that need it: turned upright, fitted to the 16:9 screen, converted to sRGB, and resized to 3840×2160.
  3. Read each TV to see what's stored on it.
  4. Upload what's missing and remove pieces you deleted from the folder.
  5. Apply TV settings such as slideshow, brightness, or power-off, only if you configured them.

The prepared version of each picture replaces it in the artwork folder, and the untouched original is kept in the folder’s hidden .frame-tv-art-manager/originals directory. Prepared versions are always re-made from the original, so changing an image setting later never costs quality.

Why you never get duplicates

A common complaint with upload scripts is the same photos piling up on the TV again and again. Frame TV Art Manager identifies each picture by a hash of its contents and keeps a per-TV record of what it uploaded. A picture that is already on the TV is never sent again, renaming a file doesn't make it new, and two identical files in the folder are stored once.

Removing art

Delete a picture from the folder and the next sync removes the copy the manager uploaded. If the folder becomes completely empty, the last piece stays on the TV so a mistake can't leave the screen blank. Art you added through SmartThings or USB is never removed unless you deliberately set REMOVE_UNKNOWN_IMAGES=true.

Several Frame TVs, one folder

List every TV in TV_IPS, separated by commas, for example TV_IPS=192.168.1.150,192.168.1.151. Each TV gets the same collection and is tracked separately, so a TV that's off doesn't hold up the others.

Adding art from your phone and the web

  • Network share: copy files into the artwork folder from any computer.
  • Upload page: turn on UPLOAD_ENABLED and open http://SERVER-IP:8080/upload in a browser. It also accepts iPhone Shortcuts and curl; see the Apple Photos guide.
  • Online sources: NASA, the Art Institute of Chicago, Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, and direct image links; see the free art guide.

What it won't do

  • Delete art you added yourself, unless REMOVE_UNKNOWN_IMAGES=true.
  • Choose which piece is on screen. The TV and its slideshow decide.
  • Change anything on a TV whose state it can't confirm. That TV is skipped until the next sync.
  • Treat a failed folder scan or a website that's down as "delete everything".
  • Change anything at all in a dry run (DRY_RUN=true).

Questions people ask

Can I upload photos to a Samsung Frame TV without the SmartThings app?

Yes. Samsung's own alternative is a USB stick. Frame TV Art Manager is another: it uploads from a folder over your local network without SmartThings or a Samsung account.

How often does Frame TV Art Manager upload new art?

At startup and then every 30 minutes by default. Change it with SYNC_INTERVAL_MINUTES.

Will it upload the same photo twice?

No. Pictures are identified by their contents, so a photo already on the TV is never uploaded again, even if you rename or re-copy the file.

Can it delete photos from the Frame TV for me?

Yes, but only the ones it uploaded. Delete the picture from the folder and the next sync removes it from the TV. Art you added another way stays.

Does it work with more than one Frame TV?

Yes. List each TV's IP address in TV_IPS, separated by commas. Every TV is kept in sync with the same folder.
