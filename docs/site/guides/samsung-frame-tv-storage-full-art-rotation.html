Storage full

Samsung Frame TV storage full? Rotate your art automatically

Short answer: The Frame stores a limited number of your own pictures, and once it's full it refuses new uploads (some models show an "Insufficient storage space" error). Frame TV Art Manager handles this by rotating: each piece it uploaded stays at least 14 days, then is swapped for art from your folder that's waiting its turn, never-shown pieces first. Art you added yourself is never touched, and no files are deleted from your folder.

Why The Frame runs out of room

Uploaded pictures live in the TV's built-in storage, which is shared with everything else on it. How many fit depends on the model, the firmware, and the file sizes, so Frame TV Art Manager doesn't guess. Without rotation, a full TV means new pictures in your folder never appear, and you're back to deleting art by hand.

Some owners have also filled their TVs with duplicates, from tools that upload the same photos again on every run. Frame TV Art Manager recognizes pictures by their contents, so it never adds a second copy.

Freeing space by hand

On the TV, open Art Mode, go to My Photos, select pictures, and delete them; the SmartThings app can do the same. That works, but it has to be repeated every time you want new art.

How automatic rotation works

The first time the TV rejects a real upload because it's full, the manager records how many pieces were on it as that TV's capacity. If your collection is bigger, rotation starts on its own, and a full TV becomes a normal, healthy state.

  • Each piece stays at least ROTATION_INTERVAL_DAYS (default 14; fractions such as 0.5 work).
  • Waiting art goes up next: never-shown pieces first, then the ones away longest. New files count as never shown.
  • A rotated-out piece doesn't come back until it has been away just as long, so a large collection works all the way through.
  • One swap at a time: each swap deletes one piece and immediately uploads its replacement, so the TV is never more than one short.

A worked example

150 pictures, a TV that holds 60, default 14-day interval
WhenWhat happens
Day 0Pictures upload until the TV refuses one as full. 60 is recorded as its capacity; 90 pictures wait.
Day 14The first 60 have had their time and are swapped, one by one, for 60 never-shown pictures.
Day 28The last 30 never-shown pictures go up, plus 30 from the first group that have been away longest.
After thatEvery picture has been shown; the cycle continues, always bringing back the art away longest.

The numbers are made up for illustration. A shorter interval cycles faster.

What rotation never touches

  • Art you added yourself through SmartThings, USB, or any other way.
  • Files in your artwork folder. Rotation only removes copies from the TV.
  • The TV's choice of what's on screen. If the displayed piece rotates out, the TV moves on to another.

If the TV is full mostly of your own pieces, rotation can't free room and the TV reports storage full. Remove some of those pieces yourself. REMOVE_UNKNOWN_IMAGES=true would also free space, but it deletes every piece the manager didn't upload, so treat it as a last resort.

Rotation and the slideshow work together

The slideshow changes which stored piece is on screen, from every 3 minutes to once a day. Rotation changes which pieces are stored, every 14 days by default. Together, a large collection keeps moving through the TV.

Checking on rotation

http://SERVER-IP:8080/status shows rotating, artwork_waiting, and next_rotation for each TV. Some TVs limit storage by size rather than count; if uploads keep failing after space was freed, rotation pauses after three failures in a row and resumes once an upload succeeds.

Set ROTATION_INTERVAL_DAYS=0 to turn rotation off. A full TV then reports storage full, keeps what's on it, and retries one upload a day, or immediately once something on the TV is removed.

Questions people ask

How many pictures can a Samsung Frame TV hold?

It depends on the model, the firmware, and the file sizes. Frame TV Art Manager learns each TV's limit the first time the TV refuses an upload because it's full.

What does the storage full error on The Frame mean?

The TV's built-in storage for your pictures is full, so it rejects new uploads until something is deleted. Frame TV Art Manager rotates its own uploads to make room automatically.

Will rotation delete my photos?

No. It only removes copies it uploaded from the TV. Files in your folder and pictures you added to the TV yourself are never deleted.

How do I stop duplicate photos filling my Frame TV?

Use an uploader that recognizes pictures by content. Frame TV Art Manager never uploads a picture that's already on the TV, even under a new name.
